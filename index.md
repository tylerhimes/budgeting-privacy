# Budgeting Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 27, 2026

Budgeting ("the app") is developed by Tyler Himes, an independent developer based in Utah, United States ("I", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

**The short version:** Budgeting has no accounts. Your budgets, expenses, savings, notes and photos are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account, and we can't see them. We collect limited, pseudonymous usage analytics to improve the app, our subscription provider processes your purchases, and free users see a privacy-friendly banner for other apps. We don't sell your data and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Your budget data

Everything you enter in Budgeting (budgets, categories, expenses, recurring payments, savings funds, notes, receipt scans and photos) is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Budgeting, Apple syncs it to your other devices through your private iCloud database. We have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is optional and only requested when you use the related feature. You can change them anytime in the Settings app.

- **Camera and Photos:** to scan receipts and attach photos to expenses. Photos are stored with your budget data as described above.
- **Microphone and Speech Recognition:** to dictate expenses. Audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition service, which may send it to Apple. We never receive your audio or transcripts.
- **Siri:** to add expenses with Siri and Shortcuts.
- **Notifications:** for reminders such as daily logging, monthly reviews, bills and savings goals. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Receipt text recognition and smart expense suggestions (using Apple Intelligence, where available) run on your device. That content isn't sent to us.

If you add a website to a recurring payment, the app loads that website to show a preview, so that site may see your IP address, just as it would if you visited it in a browser.

## Information we collect

### Usage analytics (TelemetryDeck)

We use [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. It receives events such as app launches, screens viewed, onboarding steps and error codes. During onboarding it also receives your answers to the setup questions (for example, your goal, the suggested categories you picked, and which reminders you turned on). It never receives your amounts, expense names, notes, or the names of categories you create.

Analytics events are tied to a random identifier the app generates for this device. TelemetryDeck hashes it before sending, so it doesn't identify you by name, email or account.

### Purchases (RevenueCat and Apple)

Budgeting Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information (such as device model, OS version, country and IP address). We share the hashed analytics identifier with RevenueCat so subscription and usage reports can be combined. We never receive your payment details.

### Feature requests (Kickstart Signal)

The Feature Requests board in Settings uses [Kickstart Signal](https://signal.kickstart.tools/privacy). Browsing the board doesn't create an identifier for you. If you submit a request, comment or vote, Signal receives what you wrote and a random installation ID that the app creates on your device at that point. The ID isn't derived from you, your Apple Account or your device, and is only used so your vote counts once and can be undone. Signal also receives whether you have Budgeting Pro, so we can see which requests matter to subscribers. When needed, it may also receive a temporary Apple-signed proof that the app is a genuine install.

New requests stay private until we review and publish them. **Once published, requests and comments can be seen by other users of the app**, so please don't include personal information in them. Signal doesn't use feedback for tracking, advertising or marketing.

### In-app promotion (Kickstart Exchange)

On the free tier, Budgeting shows a banner promoting other apps through [Kickstart Exchange](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy). Kickstart Exchange matches apps, not people. It receives app-level information (such as the app's identifier, version, platform and App Store country) and a temporary Apple-signed proof that the app is a genuine install. It doesn't receive your identity or a device identifier and doesn't track you. Records of which adverts are shown and tapped are kept for about 30 days and don't identify individual users or devices. Pro subscribers don't see these banners. See the [Kickstart Exchange privacy notice](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy) for details.

## How we use information

- To provide the app's features, including subscriptions.
- To understand how the app is used, find bugs, and improve it.
- To decide which features to build next, based on feature requests.
- To respond to you when you contact us.

We don't use your information for targeted advertising, we don't sell or rent it, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Sharing

We share information only:

- With the service providers named above, which process it on our behalf.
- When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users, the public, or us.
- As part of a sale or transfer of the app, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information unless you're notified otherwise.

## Retention

Your budget data stays on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it. Feature requests, comments and votes are kept on the Signal board until we remove them. Analytics and subscription data is kept by each provider according to its own retention policy and only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** delete any budget, expense, savings fund or photo.
- **From iCloud:** open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, then go to iCloud → Manage Storage (or Storage) → Budgeting and delete its data.
- **From your device:** deleting the app removes its local data.
- **Feature requests and comments:** email us with the text of the request or comment you'd like removed, and we'll delete it from the board.
- **Analytics or subscription data:** email us with the user ID shown in Budgeting's Settings, and we'll ask our providers to delete the data tied to it. Apple keeps its own purchase records, which you can manage through Apple.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to or limit how it's processed. Because we don't have accounts and don't know who you are, we'll usually need the user ID shown in Settings, or the text of your feedback, to find any data about you. To make a request, email us. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## Children

Budgeting is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
